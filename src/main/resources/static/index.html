FIELD REPORT
    Field manual · How to play

    Blue moves first. Select a blue scout or tank, choose Move or Attack, then click a highlighted tile. Each unit gets one action per turn. You can end a turn at any time. Red responds automatically.

    Scouts: 45 health, move 3 tiles, range 1, damage 18. Tanks: 75 health, move 2, range 3, damage 25. Distance uses horizontal and vertical steps; units cannot move through occupied tiles or walls. Attacks use range and can fire across walls.

    Shields: halve the next hit, then disappear; unused shields expire at that team's next turn. Recruit: choose a highlighted empty tile next to your base. New units act next turn. Maximum 8 troops per side. Relays: occupy diamond tiles for +10 energy at the start of your turn. Energy caps at 100. Destroy the enemy base to win; the match draws after round 80.
